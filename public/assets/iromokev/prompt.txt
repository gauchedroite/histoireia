# Dominus — The Pale Spire
## Game Reference Document

### Setting
A desert trading city of thieves. At its heart rises a windowless tower of pale stone — the Spire of Vorath — where a white-bearded sorcerer keeps a priceless, pulse-glowing jewel called the Pale Heart. Within the spire, chained by sorcery, dwells a tusked, four-armed being born beyond the stars, whom thieves whisper of as the Grey Sage. By night, thieves scale the wall; beasts pad in the walled garden; and a great grey spider haunts the high chambers.

## Dominus Game Rules

- **Setup**: pick/roll a PC from the Character list + name, roll the 3 Plot lists for your first plot.
- **Story**: pick/roll a Place from the Scene lists; on entering a new scene, roll a die → 3 or less = NPC encounter, 4+ = Event. Move scenes when the old one resolves.
- **Challenge**: roll a die; 4+ succeeds. Advantage = roll 2 dice keep high; disadvantage = roll 2 dice keep low.
- **Dilemma**: A/B style binary, roll d6 → 3 or less = option 1, 4+ = option 2.
- **Idea Bank**: roll for extra detail on a Place/Character/Event from the Idea Bank lists.
